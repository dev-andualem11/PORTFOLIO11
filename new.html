My Journey

Hello, I am learning web development from the very beginning. Even though I am a beginner, I have passion and potential, and I want to become a professional developer. I wanted to start from the very basics — not because I don't know anything, but because I believe in building from the foundation.

What I Really Want to Learn

  • Frontend development
  • Backend development

I used to think coding was only for geniuses. I pictured rooms full of people with advanced degrees, furiously typing cryptic symbols while the rest of us looked on in awe, convinced that this world was simply off-limits to ordinary minds like mine. Then I realized that this belief was nothing more than a story I had told myself to stay comfortable, a convenient excuse that protected me from trying something difficult and possibly failing. What actually changed my mind was seeing how many self-taught developers, career-changers, and complete beginners had built real things simply by starting small and staying curious. I began to understand that coding is not a talent you are born with but a skill you develop, one frustrating error message at a time. So I started learning to code not because I believed I was a genius, but because I finally understood that I did not need to be one.
